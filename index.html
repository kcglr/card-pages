    How prices and links work+

    Prices on the stickers

    The sticker on a sleeve is the TCGplayer market price in USD. Pull the card out to see an approximate lira conversion. Availability varies by language and printing; a sleeve without a sticker has no price yet.

    The exact printing

    Pull a card out to open its PriceCharting, Cardmarket or Collectr page. When no exact page is known you get a search instead. eBay links keep sold and active listings apart.

    Handling the binder

    Turn pages with the arrows, the page corners, your arrow keys or a swipe. Click a card to pull it out, then move over it, or press and slide on a phone, to tilt it in the light.
